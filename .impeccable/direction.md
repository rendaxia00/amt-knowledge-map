# Direction Contract — Auction Cartography

<!-- impeccable:concept-seed 5e0ce407 -->

## FORM

- **Topology:** one continuous plotted route connects five learning territories; each lesson is a coordinate on that route, not an isolated index item.
- **Material:** near-black survey ground, faint topographic contours, hairline coordinate ticks, warm bone annotations, and phase-specific mineral inks.
- **Composition:** the route bends across a tall field and alternates its stage anchors; the reading plane remains fixed and visually nearer than the drifting terrain.
- **Interaction:** choosing a coordinate foregrounds the traveled route and draws dashed relationship bearings to the chapter's conceptual neighbors.
- **Typography:** Smiley Sans is self-hosted for display and cartographic labels; readable UI/body copy uses a neutral Chinese sans stack.

## World

A late-night market cartographer's field atlas: near-black plotted terrain, warm bone editorial type, mineral teal for value/context, amber for execution, and cool blue for risk. Thin contour lines and coordinate marks create depth without decorative charts.

## First viewport

The learning map is the thesis. A fixed left instrument rail frames a large, explorable five-stage AMT decision path. The selected lesson opens as a reading sheet on the right; navigation never removes the learner from the larger system.

## Visitor path

Orient on the five-stage decision chain → choose a stage or search a concept → open a lesson → read its detailed explanation and operating checklist → follow related lessons → mark progress locally.

## Signature interaction

Selecting a lesson pulls its plotted point to the visual foreground: the route brightens, neighboring concepts remain visible, and the reader panel updates without a page reload.

## Cross-surface reach

The same plotted-point grammar supports glossary, study plans, progress, notes, and future quiz surfaces. On narrow screens, the map becomes a horizontal stage rail above the chapter reader.

## Honest risk

Dense cartographic detail can compete with reading. The interface therefore freezes background motion during reading, maintains a quiet text measure, and allows the map to collapse.

## Raised disciplines

- **Cloud quarry — spatial depth:** hierarchy is expressed as front-to-back plotted layers, not nested containers.
- **Curved crease shell — connective logic:** relation paths visibly bend between concepts instead of presenting a flat index.
- **Cracktro queue — focus stillness:** the active reading plane remains motionless while only the remote contour field drifts subtly.
- **Cutting bench — persistent place:** the last opened lesson is remembered locally and re-flagged on return.
- **Broadside — editorial density:** long explanations use decisive typographic rhythm rather than card grids.
- **Star atlas — coordinate precision:** node size, rings, labels, and relationship lines carry meaning consistently.
