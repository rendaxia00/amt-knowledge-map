# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Inferred for this session from the explicit brief: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step, so the knowledge map can be opened locally.

## Users

Inferred: a Chinese-speaking trader studying the English Cloud9 AMT course who needs a faster way to understand, revisit, and connect concepts across lessons.

## Product Purpose

Turn the English lesson subtitles under `E:\订单流课程\cloud\AMT` into a Chinese, browser-based learning atlas: an overview knowledge map plus detailed lesson-by-lesson explanations. Success means the learner can move from the AMT mental model to execution, order flow, and performance practice without repeatedly searching long videos.

## Positioning

The product connects each lesson to one coherent decision chain—context → location → trigger → risk → review—rather than presenting isolated summaries.

## Operating Context

The site is used as a local study companion alongside the course videos. It supports overview browsing, chapter navigation, topic filtering, keyword search, concept relationships, and repeatable study checklists.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Source of truth: 37 English `en.srt` subtitle files plus one lesson (#15) that has video but no English subtitle file.
- Output language: Simplified Chinese, retaining important English abbreviations such as AMT, BA, CA, DVA, VWAP, CVD, and LIS.
- Static, responsive, keyboard-accessible local website.
- Educational synthesis only; no fabricated performance claims or guaranteed trading outcomes.
- Chapter 15 is explicitly marked as title-derived because no English subtitle file is present.

## Evidence on Hand

- English subtitle corpus: `E:\订单流课程\cloud\AMT\**\en.srt` (37 files).
- Course videos and audio files in the same directory.
- Generated local corpus report: `D:\GoogleDownload\codeXWorkspace\cloud9\analysis\course_report.md`.
- No supplied logo, brand system, testimonials, or outcome data.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve the course's causal logic, not its conversational noise.
2. Show relationships before details: every chapter belongs to a larger learning path.
3. Separate observation, inference, setup, and execution.
4. Turn explanations into concrete study questions and operating checklists.
5. Label source limitations honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive layouts are required.
